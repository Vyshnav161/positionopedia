# **App Name**: Positionopedia

## Core Features:

- Position Library: Categorize and display a diverse range of sexual positions, with explicit indication of which positions allow for anal penetration.
- Step-by-step Guides: Detailed, respectful instructions on how to perform each position safely and effectively.
- Anatomical Insights: Offer anatomical information that explains how positions interact with the body, highlighting points of pleasure and areas requiring caution.
- Position Generator: AI-powered "tool" to generate position suggestions based on user-defined preferences such as flexibility, intensity, and intimacy level. Positions that do or don't accommodate anal penetration will be part of the filter criteria and results.
- Safety Disclaimer: Implement a disclaimer emphasizing the importance of consent, communication, and safe practices before accessing any content.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft coral (#F08080) to evoke warmth and intimacy without being overtly sexual.
- Background color: Light blush (#FAF0E6), offering a gentle and inviting canvas.
- Accent color: Muted lavender (#E6E6FA) to add a subtle touch of sophistication and calmness.
- Headline Font: 'Belleza', a humanist sans-serif. Body font: 'Alegreya', a serif, for readability.
- Use simple, line-drawn icons to represent categories and position types.
- Ensure a clean, spacious design that avoids feeling cluttered or overwhelming.
- Implement subtle, tasteful transitions and animations to enhance user experience without distracting from the content.
